AIMAP Lab — AI for Materials & Processing  ·  UST 소재·공정 인공지능 연구실
We build AI that designs metals, reads microstructures, predicts manufacturing processes, and runs autonomous experiments — bridging deep learning, physics-based simulation, and robotics to accelerate materials innovation.
Research Areas

01AI for Materials Design

Deep-learning property prediction and generative models that accelerate the design and discovery of new alloys.

02AI-Based Materials Analysis

AI-driven microstructure characterization, automated data analysis, and ML-based performance evaluation.

03Physics-Informed AI for Processes

Hybrid AI–simulation frameworks that embed physical constraints for accurate, reliable process prediction.

04Materials VLMs & AI Agents

Vision-language models and AI agents specialized for materials science that automate research workflows.

05Autonomous Laboratory

Robotics + AI experimental platforms for closed-loop discovery and optimization of metallic materials.

06Autonomous Manufacturing

Vision- and signal-based anomaly detection and physical AI for manufacturing systems that sense, decide, and act.
